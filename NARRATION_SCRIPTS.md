# Narration scripts

Per-section scripts for AI text-to-speech generation. Each runs ~45 seconds at natural pace (~120 words). Source-of-truth lives in `data.json` under `narration.scripts` — this file is a copy-paste convenience.

Save generated MP3s to `audio-narration/{section-id}.mp3`. The deck's player auto-detects them.

## buyers.mp3 — Buyer strategy

> There are four buyer types we'll run in parallel. Strategics — coffee operators and adjacent food and beverage companies — they tend to move quickly when the fit is right. Private equity, both platform sponsors and add-on buyers, will underwrite the repeatability of your store model. Family offices bring patient capital and tolerance for operator-led structures. International acquirers from Europe and Asia pay premiums for brand stories that travel. We run all four in parallel — the single biggest determinant of price is having multiple credible bidders at the table.

## process.mp3 — Process

> Our process has three phases over roughly twenty-six weeks. Build the story — financial cleanup, quality-of-earnings prep, your unit economics narrative. Run the field — we contact forty to sixty buyers, hold eight to twelve management meetings, and guide first-round bids to a comparable structure so price emerges rather than being negotiated. Protect the outcome — best and final tightened against benchmarks, working capital peg negotiated up front, definitive documents to signed, funded, and done.

## risks.mp3 — Risks & mitigations

> Most bankers don't talk about what could go wrong. We do. Market timing — multiples are sensitive to consumer sentiment and rates. Key-person dependence — buyers price in founder-departure risk. Channel concentration — over-reliance on one market triggers diligence friction. Working capital surprises at the peg. And diligence drag — buyers extending exclusivity to re-trade is the most common late-stage failure. For each one, we have a documented mitigation. The point is we're not selling you certainty. We're selling you preparation.

## waterfall.mp3 — Founder takeaway

> This is the slide that matters most. Headline EV is one number — your net proceeds is another. Starting from six point two million, subtract the working capital peg, existing debt, our success fee, closing costs. That gets you to equity proceeds. Multiply by your ownership stake. Subtract federal capital gains, subtract state tax. The number at the bottom — that's what hits your bank account. Drag transaction value, your ownership percent, basis, or state to see your real outcome.
